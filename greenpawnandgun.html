Pawn loans, explained

Simple, private, no credit needed

A pawn loan is one of the easiest ways to get cash when you need it. Here's how it works.

Bring in your item

Bring something of value along with a valid government-issued photo ID.

Get an offer

We evaluate your item and offer you a loan amount based on its value. No credit check.

Walk out with cash

Accept the offer and leave with cash. We hold your item securely during the loan term.

Pay back & pick up

Repay the loan plus fees before the due date and your item is yours again. You can also extend your loan.

If you choose not to repay, there's no collections, no credit damage, and no further obligation — the item simply becomes the store's. Loan terms and fees follow Utah state law; ask us for full details on your loan ticket.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need good credit to get a pawn loan?

No. Pawn loans are based entirely on the value of your item, not your credit history. We don't run credit checks, and pawn loans don't get reported to credit bureaus.

What do I need to bring?

A valid government-issued photo ID and the item you'd like to sell, trade, or pawn. Accessories and original packaging can help increase your offer.

How do you decide how much my item is worth?

We look at condition, brand, current market demand, and recent sale prices. For gold and silver, we weigh and test the metal against today's market price.

Can I extend my loan?

In most cases, yes. Come in or call before your due date and we can go over your options to extend.

What happens if I can't pay the loan back?

Nothing bad happens to you. There are no collections and no hit to your credit — the item becomes property of the store and the loan is closed.

Can you give me a quote over the phone?

We can give you a rough idea for many items over the phone, but we'll need to see it in person for an exact offer.
