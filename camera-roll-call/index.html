Your friends'iPhones

A photo you approve travels from your phone to the other phones in the game over an encrypted local connection, is shown for one round, and is gone. It never passes through a server, because there isn't one.

Who this is for

For a group of friends or family in the same room who want something better to do with their phones than scroll them side by side. Everyone already carries years of photos; this turns them into a game where knowing your friends is the whole skill.

It will suit you if party apps you've tried wanted an account, a subscription, or your photos on somebody's server. It will not suit you if your group isn't in the same place: it only works between phones that are near each other, by design.

How to play

  1. Get everyone in the same room with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. You don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. One person taps Host a game and reads out the 4-letter code. Everyone else taps Join with a code.
  3. Each player draws a random batch from their camera roll, taps away anything they'd rather keep private, and approves the rest.
  4. The host taps Start game. A photo appears on every phone. Pick whose it is before the clock runs out. Fast and right scores big, and fooling your friends with your own photos scores too.
  5. Play again and the next person hosts, so no single phone does all the work.

What it does

Photos, Video mode, or a mix

Play with photos, short clips from your videos, or both. The host picks, and changing it asks everyone to approve again, because that changes what they agreed to share.

You approve everything

The game shows you a random batch first. Tap any photo to leave it out, draw a new batch, or plant a specific one with the system picker. You can pull your photo even mid-round.

Up to 16 players

Two is enough to try it. Three or more is a real game, because each round then has decoy names to fool you.

The host decides the rules

Rounds, timers, number of choices, scoring, streak bonuses, and presets for Quick, Classic, Marathon and Hard. Hard mode starts each photo blurred and sharpens it as the clock runs down.

Step away and come back

Switch apps for a moment and the game keeps your place. If your connection drops, your phone rejoins the room on its own.

Report and block

Report anything that shouldn't be there and it disappears from every phone at once. Block a player and you'll never see each other's photos again.

Cleaned before it's shared

Photos are resized and re-encoded before they leave your phone, which strips location and camera details. Clips are cut to a few seconds and re-encoded the same way.

Nothing to buy, and nothing sold

No adverts, no purchases, no subscription, no analytics and no tracking.
