Privacy Policy — Camera Roll Call

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Camera Roll Call is a party game for people in the same room. Each player approves a few photos or videos from their own library, and the group guesses whose each one is. This policy covers everything the app does with them.

The short version: nothing is collected. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. A photo or clip goes only to the other phones in your game, and only if you approved it.

Data the developer collects

None. There is:

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing counts what you play, who you play with, or when.
  • No servers, no SDKs and no advertising networks. The app never contacts the internet to play a game.
  • No ads, no purchases, no subscription.

The app is declared as "Data Not Collected" on its App Store privacy label.

What the app reads on your phone

Your photo library. To draw a random batch for you to review, the app reads your library on your phone. You can give it full access or limited access, or give it none and pick photos one at a time with the system picker, which hands over only the photo you chose.

The local network. The app uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity to find nearby phones in the same game, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. iOS asks your permission the first time.

Your display name. The name you type is kept on your phone so you don't have to retype it, and shown to the other players in your game.

What is shared, and with whom

  • Only what you approve is ever shared, and only with the other players in your current game.
  • It is cleaned first. Photos are resized and re-encoded, and videos are cut to a short clip and re-encoded. This removes location, camera details and timestamps.
  • It goes phone to phone. Items travel directly between phones over an encrypted local connection. They are never sent to me or to any server, because there is none.
  • It is gone after the round. Other phones hold a photo in memory, or a clip in a protected temporary file, only while its round is on screen. Both are deleted when the round ends, and leftover files are cleared when the app starts or you leave a game.
  • Screenshots are possible. Other players can take a screenshot or screen recording of what's on their screen. The app can't prevent that, so only approve what you're comfortable showing the people in the room.

What is kept on your phone

Your display name, the host's game settings, a random player ID (so a block keeps working between games), and the list of players you've blocked. None of it leaves your phone, except your name, which the players in your game see. Deleting the app removes all of it.

iCloud Photos

If a photo you approve is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple's iCloud to your phone before it can be shared. That is handled by Apple, not by Camera Roll Call.

Reporting and blocking

Reports and blocks happen entirely on the players' phones. A report removes the item from every phone in the game at once. Nothing about a report or a block is sent to me.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
