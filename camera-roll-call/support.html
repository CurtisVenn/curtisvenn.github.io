Getting a game going

  1. Get everyone in the same room with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. You don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. One person taps Host a game and shares the 4-letter code. Everyone else taps Join with a code.
  3. Each player approves a few photos or videos, then taps I'm ready.
  4. The host taps Start game.

Common questions

My friends can't find my room

Check that everyone has Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on in Settings (not just Control Center), and stand close together for the first join. Then check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Camera Roll Call is on. If someone tapped Don't Allow when the app first asked, their phone won't see any rooms until this is turned on.

It found the room but couldn't connect

Move closer to the host and try again. The app retries on its own a few times before it gives up. Low Power Mode can make the first connection slower.

I switched apps and came back

The game keeps your place. If you were away long enough for the connection to drop, you'll see Reconnecting… and your phone will rejoin by itself. If the host was away, everyone rejoins once the host opens the app again.

It says it can't see my photos

Go to Settings → Camera Roll Call → Photos and choose Full Access or Limited Access. You can also plant photos one at a time without giving access.

How many people can play?

From 2 to 16 in one room. Three or more is more fun, because each round then has decoy names.

Someone shared something they shouldn't have

Tap … during the round and choose Report this. It's removed from every phone right away. To stop seeing someone's photos in future games, choose Block.

How do I unblock someone?

On the home screen, tap Blocked players.

Is anything uploaded?

No. There are no accounts and no servers. See the privacy policy.

Still stuck?

Email help@plainandsimple.app with what you tried and what the app said, and which iPhones you were using.
